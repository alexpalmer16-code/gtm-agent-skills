# Writing standards

Write for sales leaders and commercial operators. Use concrete observations, decisions, tradeoffs and consequences. Prefer ordinary words and connected sentences.

- Use Australian English. Use zero em dashes. Rewrite sentences rather than substituting double hyphens.
- Avoid repeated binary contrasts, slogan fragments, generic AI hooks, forced three-part lists and engagement bait.
- Distinguish sourced facts, the author's observation, inference, prediction and illustrative examples.
- Never turn an illustrative scenario into Alex's personal experience. Do not invent customer outcomes or time savings.
- Link evidence that supports the specific claim. Flag missing evidence before drafting stronger claims.
- Preserve the author's actual degree of certainty. An instruction to sound confident cannot repair weak evidence.

For article interviews, ask one question at a time. Follow up on vague answers. Confirm the central argument, usable evidence and privacy boundaries before drafting. A useful article contains a decision, tradeoff, experiment or prediction Alex is prepared to defend.
