# First publication cycle

## Build one: meeting preparation

Run the supplied synthetic case. Then trial with a cleared or private real meeting brief outside this repository. Compare the output with the preparation Alex would actually take into a meeting. Record errors and useful changes, not just whether the output sounds polished.

Related article: **A practical AI meeting-preparation workflow**. Explain inputs, source checks, human judgment and the manager's quality check. Include measured results only after a real trial. Link the published skill revision so readers can reproduce the workflow.

## Build two: buying-window review

Exercise contradictory signals, stale evidence and absent buyer confirmation. Test whether the skill recommends proportionate discovery rather than inventing purchase intent.

Related article: **Building a buying-window engine**. Initially describe this skill as a manual signal review. An engine would additionally need ingestion, freshness rules, repeated evaluation and an operating owner. Do not imply these exist yet.

## Third skill candidate

Pipeline inspection, once stage definitions and a synthetic opportunity dataset are available. Agree cohort, denominator, currency, snapshot date and data completeness before interpreting changes.

## Sustainable weekly session

With evidence ready: 10 minutes of voice interview, 20 minutes reviewing a skill output, 20 minutes editing the article, 10 minutes inspecting the diff and approving a revision. New research and experiments may need separate time. Publish when the evidence and review are ready.

The loop: observation, thesis, tested workflow, website article, channel adaptations, objections and reported use, next experiment. Keep private interview notes outside this public repository. Prepare distribution copy only after approving the central article.
